Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

YT Studio Reader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the app reads YouTube channel and video information and YouTube Analytics reports (for example views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscriber counts) for channels the owner manages.

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure and improve the owner's own videos. It is stored on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Stored reports can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the app then stops reading data immediately.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
